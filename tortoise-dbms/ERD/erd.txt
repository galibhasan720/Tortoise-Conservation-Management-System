1. The Finalized Entities & Attributes

A. Core & Gateway Entities

ROLES: role_id (PK), role_name.

USERS: user_id (PK), role_id (FK), username, password_hash.

SPECIES_DICT [NEW]: species_id (PK), species_name, conservation_status. (This prevents typos and normalizes your data).

ENCLOSURE: enclosure_id (PK), capacity, habitat_type, location.

TORTOISE_PROFILE [UPDATED]: tortoise_id (PK), enclosure_id (FK), species_id (FK) [UPDATED], gender, weight, date_of_entry, origin_location [NEW], status.

B. Operational & Transactional Entities

FEEDING_LOG: log_id (PK), tortoise_id (FK), user_id (FK), food_type, amount, timestamp.

HEALTH_RECORD: record_id (PK), tortoise_id (FK), user_id (FK), diagnosis, treatment_plan, vaccination_date.

BREEDING_RECORD: breeding_id (PK), male_tortoise_id (FK), female_tortoise_id (FK), user_id (FK), mating_date, egg_count, hatch_success.

TASK_SCHEDULE: task_id (PK), user_id (FK), description, due_date, status.

C. Environmental & Automated Entities

IOT_TELEMETRY: telemetry_id (PK), enclosure_id (FK), temperature, humidity, timestamp.

ALERTS [UPDATED]: alert_id (PK), enclosure_id (FK), trigger_reason, timestamp, status, resolved_by_user_id (FK) [NEW]. (Tracks exactly which Environmental Tech fixed the issue).

2. The Complete Cardinality Mapping (For your ERD)
When you draw your ER Diagram, use these exact relationships. I have added the missing 1-to-N relationships for the transactional tables.

User & Access Relationships:

ROLES (1) to USERS (N): One role (e.g., "Caretaker") belongs to many users.

USERS (1) to FEEDING_LOG (N): One Caretaker can log many feedings.

USERS (1) to HEALTH_RECORD (N): One Vet can write many health records.

USERS (1) to BREEDING_RECORD (N): One Breeding Officer logs many breeding events.

USERS (1) to TASK_SCHEDULE (N): One user can be assigned multiple tasks.

USERS (1) to ALERTS (N): One Environmental Tech can resolve many alerts.

Tortoise & Habitat Relationships:

SPECIES_DICT (1) to TORTOISE_PROFILE (N): One species classification applies to many individual tortoises.

ENCLOSURE (1) to TORTOISE_PROFILE (N): One enclosure holds many tortoises.

ENCLOSURE (1) to IOT_TELEMETRY (N): One enclosure generates thousands of telemetry data points over time.

ENCLOSURE (1) to ALERTS (N): One enclosure can trigger multiple environmental alerts.

Tortoise Action Relationships:

TORTOISE_PROFILE (1) to FEEDING_LOG (N): One tortoise has many meals recorded over its lifetime.

TORTOISE_PROFILE (1) to HEALTH_RECORD (N): One tortoise has many vet visits.

TORTOISE_PROFILE (1) to BREEDING_RECORD (N) [MALE]: One male tortoise can be part of many breeding records.

TORTOISE_PROFILE (1) to BREEDING_RECORD (N) [FEMALE]: One female tortoise can be part of many breeding records.
